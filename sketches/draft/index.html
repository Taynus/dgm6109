Cooking Mastery: Data Visualization Proposals

Data Collection Overview

In this project, I am analyzing my cooking habits to find correlations between the time spent in the kitchen, my technical mastery of a dish, and my emotional well-being. I have collected data on the following variables:

  • Time: Duration of the cooking session in minutes (Estimated range: 0-120).
  • Mastery Level: My perceived skill or success with the dish (Scale: 1-5).
  • Mood: My emotional state during the process (Scale: 1-5).
  • Food Type: Categorical data including Meat, Legumes, Salad, Soup, and Other.

Proposal 1: Scatterplot Variant

Hypothesis: As the time spent cooking increases, my mastery level also increases. Better moods are generally associated with longer, more successful cooking sessions.

Data Elements: Time (X-axis), Mastery Level (Y-axis), and Mood (represented by bubble size).

Units & Range: Time (0-120 minutes), Mastery (1-5), Mood (1-5).

Proposal 2: Parallel Coordinates Plot

Hypothesis: There is a positive correlation where higher time values align with higher mastery and elevated mood scores.

Data Elements: Time, Mastery, and Mood.

Units & Range: Time (0-120 min), Mastery (1-5), Mood (1-5).

Representation: Each individual cooking session is represented by a line connecting the specific values across three vertical axes.

Proposal 3: Creative "Tetris" Block Visualization

Research Question: How does the specific category of food prepared influence my mood and the time I invest in cooking?

Data Elements: Mood (X-axis), Time (Y-axis), Mastery Level (represented by shape), and Food Type (represented by color).

Units & Range: Mood (1-5), Time (0-120 min), Mastery (5 shapes), Food Type (5 categories).
