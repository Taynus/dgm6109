Culinary Evolution: Analyzing Cooking Time and Skill Development

Hypothesis: As my daily time spent cooking increases, my kitchen mastery levels also increase.

Beyond the primary relationship between time and mastery, this visualization incorporates Creative Mindset (Mood) as a key factor. Using an X-axis scale from 1 to 5, I track how emotional states impact the duration of meal preparation. Furthermore, Dish Categories are color-coded to identify if specific ingredients, such as legumes or pasta, correlate with higher technical complexity regardless of the time invested.

  • The "High Mood" Efficiency: On days with a Mood Score of 5/5, I achieved Level 5 Mastery (Z shape) in several pasta dishes within only 20-26 minutes, suggesting that a positive mindset accelerates technical execution.
  • Low Complexity Stalness: Meat-based dishes like "Tuna Fajitas" show a lack of correlation between time and skill; they consistently remain at Level 1 or 2 Mastery even when prepared frequently, indicating these recipes offer less room for technical growth.
  • Conclusion: While time is a factor, Mastery is more closely tied to the type of dish and my creative disposition. My hypothesis is partially supported: time increases mastery primarily when the cook is in a highly creative mindset.
